THE VIGILANTS — PLAYER CHRONICLE

Earth-676 • New York City • Nov 1976 to Jan 1977

Updated: 2025-11-06



This is a spoiler-free recap of what your heroes experienced on screen. Dates are in-game.



TEAM ROSTER (SO FAR)

**Astrid Stormbringer** — Asgardian with super-strength, flight, wind/lightning powers.

**Counter-Strike** — A mutant heir to a fortune and possessing Ultimate Fighting skill and the power to Nullify.

**Resilient** — An altered human with powers of: Plasticity, Body Armor, Leaping, and Absorption Power. Early sessions.

**Screamwave** — A mutant teenager with Sonic powers of: Absorption, Energy Touch, Sound Manipulation, Flight and Sound Generation. Joined mid-January 1977

**Wartorn** — An Alien, the avatar of a Celtic god with powers Regeneration, Electrical Manipulation, Body Armor, and Shocking Touch. Joined mid-January 1977



Recurring allies: **Felicia Frost** (aka Psi-ren), **Johnny Frost** (aka Crosswise), Curator Maximilian Pride



SESSION LOG



(11/12/24) Nov 12, 1976 — Blackout and Break-In

During a rolling NYC blackout, Astrid, Counter-Strike, and Resilient cross paths and stop a jewelry-store robbery. The team-to-be wins its first victory together.



(11/26/24) Nov 13, 1976 — ESU Break-In: Mercs and Masks

Astrid and Counter-Strike interrupt a robbery at an ESU lab, tangling with **Whiplash, Boomerang**, and **A.I.M. agents**. The crew is arrested; their target was specialized tech components.



(11/29/24) Approx. Nov 16, 1976 — “Hulk” on the News

Reports of the Hulk rampaging pull the duo into action. It is a robot, not the real deal. **Spider-Man** swings in to assist. The machine is disabled; Astrid salvages a peculiar custom chip.



(11/29/24) Nov 19, 1976 -- The Met Robbery

The Metropolitan Museum announces the theft of an old tome, “**A Madman’s Mutterings**.” The heroes meet Curator Maximilian Pride, who asks for help and offers a reward of $50,000 for recovering the artifact.



(11/29/24) Nov 19, 1976 -- Dusk in the Park (Felicia)

Outside the Met, Felicia Frost (who is a waitress at the Met cafe) quietly asks for an escort through the park and confides her fear about her twin brother Johnny and how he is mixed up with the Flashing Blades gang. She thinks Johnny had something to do with the theft of the Madman's Mutterings book. She begs the heroes to steer him away from crime, telling them that a bar named Hank’s in Times Square is where they hang out. The heroes promise to help him reform, not jail him.



(11/29/24) Nov 19, 1976 -- Midnight at Hank’s Bar (Times Square)

At Hank’s Tavern, a werewolf is beaten but vanishes in a strange flash. The heroes save Johnny from danger and foil a drive-by by gunmen. The name “Hammerhead” surfaces on the street.



(12/20/24) Dec 3, 1976 -- Headlines and Nightmares

After a brief lull in civilian life, the team stops both the **Shroud** and **Frankenstein’s Monster** as “monster” headlines sweep the city.



(12/20/24) Dec 17, 1976 — Lovers at the Arch

At Washington Square, skeletons claw up from the ground as multiple costumed foes arrive. The team rescues two teen lovers and defeats several villains (Scarecrow, Gypsy Moth, Black Talon, Brothers Grimm) who blink out mid-capture. The arch stands unharmed.



(12/20/24) Dec 18, 1976 — Giving Season

A whirlwind of charity appearances—a toy drive, a food drive, and a tree-lighting—ends with **Electro** attempting to rob the crowd. He is thwarted by Astrid and Counter-Strike, but slips away.



(12/20/24) Dec 31, 1976 — On the Waterfront

At a waterfront warehouse, a masked villain called **Mister Fear** riles up mixed street gangs beside crates of assault rifles. The heroes secure the guns, protect Johnny, and scatter the mob, but the mastermind escapes in another flash.



(1/31/25) Jan 1, 1977 -- Martinez Construction — The Crane Incident (Astrid solo)

A failing tower crane threatens dozens at the Martinez Construction Site. Astrid stabilizes the swing and evacuates civilians before anything catastrophic can happen.



(2/20/25) Jan 1, 1977 -- Night Moves at the Disco

Following leads into the nightclub scene, Astrid and Counter-Strike uncover a Roxxon-linked crew. They overrun on-site personnel and leave with new questions about corporate fingerprints on the street wars.



(3/14/25) Jan 3–5, 1977 — An Old Flame / Family Debt (Astrid solo)

Under her civilian identity Tess Winters, Astrid learns Joey Morelli (brother to her boss Frank Morelli) is in Maggia debt. Through subterfuge and legwork she erases the debt and secures Joey’s safety. Astrid finds the inert body of the original Human Torch under a tarp in the basement of Morelli's Electronics Repair shop and begins to work on him.



(3/19/25) Jan 7, 1977 — Grand Hotel Charity Ball

A high-society charity gala brings goodwill and masks into the same ballroom. The Vigilants keep the peace and donors’ wallets where they belong. Resilient makes a rare appearance, helping the team take down the bad guys: Maggia thugs and the supervillain Scorpion are defeated and arrested.



(5/1/25) Jan 13, 1977 -- Investigating Bellevue Hospital

Clues point the team toward industrial-chemical incidents and zombie-like attackers. Screamwave enters the fray, pairing with Astrid as the investigation deepens. The duo talk to a Bellevue doctor named Dr. List.



(5/1/25) Jan 13, 1977 — The Obelisk

At the Central Park obelisk, Felicia behaves oddly and sends Astrid and Screamwave to Johnny's apartment. It feels like a setup, but the heroes press on.



(5/1/25) Jan 13, 1977 — Johnny’s Apartment (Bomb)

The apartment is booby-trapped. The bomb detonates. Astrid’s wind shield keeps Screamwave alive; both heroes survive.



(5/1/25) Jan 13, 1977 — Chemical Factory Rescue

Inside a chemical plant, zombie attackers swarm as Johnny hangs upside down above a toxic vat. Astrid and Screamwave fight through and pull him to safety.



(5/15/25) Jan 14, 1977 — A Message in the Personals

Unable to contact masked heroes directly, Johnny Frost leaves a Daily Bugle personals message to set a safe meeting.



(5/15/25) Jan 14, 1977 — Ellis Island, Part I

Johnny tells the heroes, who have been joined by newcomer Wartorn, that he has had visions of the Statue of Liberty and that his twin sister Felicia is being held there. An underground base full of Sentinels is discovered. The heroes are saved by Black Panther, who had been trailing the heroes in his advanced jet.



(5/15/25) Jan 14, 1977 — Ellis Island, Part II (“Wide Awake”)

Astrid, Screamwave, and Wartorn return to Ellis Island to finish the job. This time, they are armed with an EMP device, courtesy of Black Panther. They temporarily disable the Sentinels, fight various guards and scientists, find and rescue Felicia who had been held prisoner.



(5/29/25) Jan 21, 1977 AM, ch. 28: A Message from Max -- The super heroes re­ceive a message from Mr. Maximilian Pride, the curator of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, placed in the personals section of the Daily Bugle.



(5/29/25) Jan 21, 1977 AM, ch. 29: To the Met Once More -- The heroes meet Mr. Pride at the Metropolitan Museum of Art. Mr. Pride has been extensively researching Bartholo mew Jacobs, the author of A Madman's Mutterings. He has uncovered some important information. He opens a copy of The Spirit Tracker, an obscure newspaper dealing with the supernatural, and reads an article to you:

"Several students of New York University report having seen a ghost beneath the Washington Square Arch at the stroke of midnight last night.



(5/29/25) Jan 21, 1977 AM, ch. 30: The Burning Ghost -- The heroes encounter the ghost of Bartholomew Jacobs.

Bartholomew Jacobs has returned to haunt the arch because it was here that Black Talon used the book to raise the dead from their graves. At the present time, the ghost cannot venture more than 1 area away from the arch. 

&nbsp; Although he is in obvious pain, Bartholomew can speak to the he­ roes. He informs them that he has come to reclaim his book. However, he cannot leave the flames unless his spirit enters a mortal body. If one of the heroes will accept his spirit, Bartholomew promises to guide them to the book.

&nbsp; Unfortunately, Bartholomew is a liar. When one of the heroes accepts the offer and approaches the ghost, Bartholomew extends his hand from the spectral flames and touches the character. The spirit of the hero and the spirit of Bartholomew transfer­ the hero's soul is now fixed to the burning pole, and Bartholomew's soul is in possession of the character's body.



(5/29/25) Jan 21, 1977 AM, ch. 31: The World Below -- The heroes follow Bartholomew Jacobs, whose spirit possesses the body of Astrid Stormbringer, into the sewers below Manhattan. You follow Astrid down a long steel ladder into the main sewer tunnels of the city. Vapors rise from a stream of foul sewage running down the middle of the tunnel. Through the murky air you can see numerous pipes intersecting at odd angles. Not only does the sewer smell bad, but it is also a bewildering maze.

As your possessed comrade heads off down a walkway above the fetid water, her footsteps echo eerily off the grimy walls. From her familiar face comes the voice of Bartholomew Jacobs: "Follow me, quickly. I feel that time is short!"

The heroes fight off alligators. Later, they stumble upon a large drug processing lab where they defeat six gang members. They continue to follow their possessed teammate, who leads them into the Morlock tunnels.



(5/29/25) Jan 21, 1977 AM, ch. 32: Morlocks and Marauders -- The heroes meet two of the denizens of the Morlock tunnels,

&nbsp;while Bartholomew Jacobs faces the wrath of the rulers of the nether realms.



*Your descent through the sewers has led you to a different network of tunnels. These are obviously not meant for sewage, but were designed with some other purpose in mind.*

*You have entered a vast tunnel that is at least 50 feet wide and 50 feet high. The walls are lined with pipes, bulges, and protrusions of all kinds. Their function is not immediately apparent.*

*As you study your new environment, you see a flash of light down the tunnel and hear a scream of pain. A battle is going on nearby!*



Skids and Arclight. The battle wit­ nessed by the heroes is between one of the Morlocks, named Skids, and one of the Marauders, called Ar­ clight. When the characters ap­ proach the fight, read the following:



You see two women engaged in a fierce battle. One of them is a teenager, while the other appears to be around forty. The young girl is on the defen­sive, struggling to protect herself with a weak force field. The older woman seems intent on killing her, as she sends the girl flying into the wall of the tunnel with a 
powerful shockwave attack.



(5/29/25) Jan 21, 1977 AM, ch. 33: Within the Nether Realm, Astrid's soul endures great suffering. The only way to escape is to make a bargain with the rulers.

  *You are chained to a massive pillar of black stone. It must be hundreds of miles high. The atmosphere all about you crackles and sparks with crimson flames.*

  *Occasional gouts of fire spiral up the stone column. Their touch causes your soul to shudder in agony.*

*You are not alone in your mis­ery. Looking up and down the. length of the black pillar, you see
tens of thousands of screaming souls chained with you.*

  *Time has no meaning in this place. It is impossible to tell how long you have been here, but it seems an eternity.*

  *Without warning, the voice that spoke in the sewer booms out.*

  *"I hope you are enjoying your stay in my domain. I have come to make you a bargain."*

In exchange for her soul, Astrid must destroy Bartholomew's book for the rulers of this domain. Being immortal, they are in no hurry and thus no time limit is set. To fulfill her part of the bar­gain, the player character merely have to cast the grimoire into a fire.

&nbsp;

The super hero cannot argue the matter. When the character accepts the bargain, her soul is returned to her body.





(6/6/25) Jan 21, 1977 AM, ch. 34: The Rescue of Callisto -- Callisto has been captured by Marauders. If the heroes rescue her, she and Skids lead them to Mister Fear.



(6/6/25) Jan 21, 1977 AM, ch. 35: The Defeat of Mister Fear -- The heroes discover the hideout of the Faces of Fear.
